## What this workflow tests

`poster` is a workflow whose design rests on **cited evidence** — the brief, the director's documents and one
`Research` run on the subject — with a `direction.md` that cites it line by line, marks what is assumed, and the print
file with its checks (`outPdf`). Reach for `polson://manual/14` §3a for the print file, `polson://sdk/core/Research`
and `polson://sdk/core/Documents` for the evidence, and `polson://manual/09` and `/11` for composition and type.

Answer these in `findings.md`:

- **Did the research carry the subject?** Which fields came back, at what confidence, and which did you take to the
  director or mark assumed? Did it settle which film was meant, or what kind of film the comparables made it? Where
  did it contradict what you had from memory?
- **What did the evidence change?** Name two choices that came from the brief, a document or the research and would
  have been different without it. If you cannot, say so — that is the finding this workflow most needs.
- **Where did the evidence run out?** Which choices had no brief, document or research behind them, and how did you
  decide them? How many rows of `direction.md` are *assumed*?
- **Was `Research` usable as described?** Did one run carry everything; did you start it with `wait: false` and
  collect it before the concepts; did anything get refused, and was the refusal useful?
- **The print checks.** Which `[WARN]` lines fired, which did you fix, and which did you keep and why?
  Were any of them wrong?
- **The distance renders.** Did the far render change what you did? Was the arithmetic in the
  instructions enough to make one, or did you have to work it out?
- **The director's questions.** Which did you ask, which did you assume, and was the list the right one?
- **The concepts.** Were your three genuinely different — dominant element, composition, palette — or one idea three
  ways? Which item of the evidence started each, and why did the chosen one win?
- **A product ad** (type `product`). What did the research say about the category, and did it give you anything to
  design from? The packshot: one generation, the same can in every view, no stray
  lettering, and did your label read on it? Quote the claim as printed.
  Quote the descriptor as printed, and the cold viewing's answer for each ad word for word. Did any ad fail it at
  first, and what did you change?
- **The set.** Did the design hold across the versions — and did anything move besides the words, the numerals and
  the reading direction, and why? For each language, how many lines were rewritten after the back-translation?
